A TRAVEL COMPANION, NOT JUST A GUIDE

Built around the decisions you make while traveling through Italy.

A trip through Italy can move between cities, stations, walking routes, restaurants, museums, weather changes and last-minute decisions in a single day. 687 Italy keeps these parts connected so the app remains useful after the planning stage is over.

BEFORE YOU GO

Build the structure.

Create the itinerary, organize destinations and stops, keep your budget in view and prepare the practical parts of the trip before departure.

WHILE YOU TRAVEL

Use the place you are in.

Nearby, translation and navigation help you move from discovery to action without leaving the rest of your trip context behind.

WHEN THINGS CHANGE

Adapt the day.

NOW, Intelligent Day, Prevent, Guardian and Signals help you understand what still makes sense when timing, weather or plans change.

PLAN ITALY AS ONE CONNECTED JOURNEY

Flights, trains, hotels, reservations and multiple cities — all part of the same trip.

An Italy trip often links airports, high-speed rail, regional trains, hotels, museum reservations and several cities. 687 Italy keeps these fixed pieces connected to the daily itinerary so Rome, Florence, Venice, Milan, Naples or smaller stops can belong to one understandable route.

Flights & airport daysKeep arrival and departure times connected to transfers and the first or last travel day.
Trains between citiesPlan city-to-city rail stages as part of the route, not as separate notes.
Hotels by destinationKeep each stay attached to the city and dates it belongs to.
Timed reservationsProtect museum entries, tours, restaurants and other fixed commitments inside the day.
Multi-city itinerariesConnect several Italian destinations without losing the overall structure of the trip.
Car rental & road daysInclude driving stages and move from the plan to supported navigation when needed.
