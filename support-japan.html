Last updated: October 27, 2026

If something is not working as expected, these quick checks solve many common issues.

Location / Japan tools

  • Allow Location permission for 687 Japan.
  • The live map and Nearby work only when your GPS location is in Japan. Outside Japan, Itinerary and the other planning tools remain available.
  • If the location seems wrong, close and reopen the app and check that location is turned on in your phone settings.

Translate, Conversation and voice

  • Allow Microphone permission and select your speaking language before using the microphone.
  • On Android, use Google Chrome: the Japanese voice may not work in other browsers.
  • If you hear no Japanese voice, install the Japanese voice in your phone settings: Settings → Text-to-speech output → Google → Install voice data → Japanese.

Guardian notifications

  • Open Guardian and tap Enable Guardian, then allow notifications.
  • Use Send test notification to check that alerts reach your phone.
  • If notifications do not arrive, check that notifications for 687 Japan (or Chrome) are allowed in your phone settings.

Maps or external booking pages

  • Make sure you have an internet connection.
  • Tap the booking or navigation button again if the external provider did not finish loading.
  • Prices, availability and booking pages are controlled by the external provider.

Old version still showing

On Android, the app may briefly display cached content after an update. Close and reopen the app; if necessary, clear only the app or Chrome cache and try again.

Your data

Use Tools → Delete trip & local data to remove locally stored trip information from the device. Privacy details: go687.com/privacy-japan.html.
