ABOUT 687

Independent, practical travel help for Italy and Japan.

go687.com publishes free travel guides and builds country-focused travel apps. Here is who we are and how we work.

Who we are

687 is an independent project created by Gabriel Potroca, an independent developer based in Italy. It started from a simple frustration: travel information is scattered across dozens of sites and apps, and the most useful details — a ZTL gate, a train strike, the right phrase in a Japanese restaurant — are usually found too late.

go687.com brings those answers together in clear, practical guides, and the 687 Italy and 687 Japan apps keep the same help available during the trip.

How we write our guides

  • Practical first: each guide answers a real question travelers ask, with steps you can follow on the day.
  • Checked against official sources: rail operators, airports, city councils and tourism boards. Rules and prices change, so always confirm on the official site before you travel.
  • Updated: we review guides when rules, fares or services change.
  • Independent: no company pays to appear in our guides or to change what we write.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on go687.com and in our apps are affiliate links. If you book or buy through them, 687 may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions help keep the guides free. We only link services that are relevant to the guide, and affiliate partnerships never decide what we recommend.
