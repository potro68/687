687 Japan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

687 Japan is a travel-planning app that helps you organize a trip to Japan before and during travel. This policy applies to the 687 Japan app on Android and iOS and to the web app at tripjpn.app.

Information stored on your device

  • Trip details such as dates, route, cities, budget and planned expenses.
  • Travel Wallet documents you choose to add.
  • App preferences and saved or recoverable trip data.

This information is stored locally on your device. You can remove local trip data from the app's Tools section, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Location

With your permission, 687 Japan uses your device location for features such as GPS verification, Nearby places, the live map, weather, maps and navigation. Location access is controlled by your device settings and can be turned off at any time.

Microphone and speech

If you use voice input in Translate, Conversation mode or the Voice Assistant, microphone and speech-recognition access is used to transcribe the words you choose to speak. Text-to-speech uses your device's voices. Permissions remain under your device control.

Translation

Text you choose to translate is sent to a translation service through the 687 server so the translation can be returned to you. It is not used to identify you.

Guardian notifications and Signals

Guardian is optional and off until you enable it. If you enable it, the app sends to the 687 server:

  • a random device identifier created by the app and the push-notification subscription of your browser or device;
  • your alert preferences (weather, leave time, route, signals);
  • trip context needed for alerts, such as your approximate location and locality, the next planned stop and the time of a planned commitment.

This information is used only to send you travel alerts. You can pause Guardian in the app at any time and turn off notifications in your device settings.

If you view or report travel Signals, the location of the area and the signal you report are sent to the 687 server so they can be shown to other travelers nearby. Signals do not include your name or contact details.

External services

687 Japan can open third-party services for travel searches, booking, maps, weather, nearby places and other travel functions (for example Google Maps). Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may process information according to their own terms.

Bookings and payments

687 Japan does not process travel payments or store payment-card details. Purchases are completed directly with external providers.

Affiliate links

Some external travel links may be affiliate links. If you make a qualifying purchase, 687 may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Children

687 Japan is a general travel utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to support@go687.com or visit go687.com/support-japan.html.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The current version is always published at go687.com/privacy-japan.html.
