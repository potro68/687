How to validate a train ticket in Italy

The one step many visitors miss. Which tickets need stamping, how to do it in ten seconds, and what to do if the machine is broken.

Updated 8 November 2026 · 6 min read · By the 687 team
IN THIS GUIDEShort answerWhich ticketsHow to validateDigital check-inForgot or brokenBuses & metroUseful words

The short answer

Paper regional tickets without a fixed time must be validated before you board. High-speed tickets (Frecciarossa, Frecciargento, Italo) and digital regional tickets do not need a machine — but digital regional tickets may need a check-in in the app.

Forgetting to validate is the most common fine for visitors in Italy. Inspectors can charge around €50 or more on the spot, even if you bought a valid ticket.

Which tickets need validation

  • Paper regional ticket (Regionale) from a machine or ticket office: yes, always validate it before boarding.
  • Frecciarossa, Frecciargento, Frecciabianca, Italo, Intercity: no. They are tied to a specific train, date and seat.
  • Digital regional ticket (Trenitalia app or website): no machine, but if it has no fixed time you must do the check-in in the app before departure.
  • Contactless payment at the gate (on some lines, e.g. Malpensa Express and some urban routes): tap the same card in and out — that is your validation.

How to validate a paper ticket

  1. Find the validation machine (obliteratrice): small green-and-white or yellow boxes near the platforms or at the start of each track.
  2. Insert the ticket in the slot with the printed side facing you, in the direction of the arrow.
  3. Wait for the click: the machine prints the date, time and station on the ticket.
  4. Check the stamp is readable, then board.

Validated regional tickets are usually valid for 4 hours from the stamp for the route printed on them (check the conditions on your ticket).

Check-in for digital regional tickets

If you buy a regional ticket in the Trenitalia app or website without a specific train, open the ticket and tap Check-in before boarding. If you choose a specific train when buying, the ticket is usually already valid for that train only — check what the ticket screen says.

Machine broken or you forgot?

  • Machine not working: try another one on a different platform. If none works, write the date, time and station on the ticket by hand and tell the conductor as soon as you board.
  • Forgot to validate: look for the conductor immediately, before they find you. Being proactive often avoids the fine, though it is at their discretion.
  • Never: validate the same ticket twice or travel on a different route than printed.

Buses, trams and metro

City transport works the same way: paper tickets must be stamped in the machine on board or at the metro gate. In many cities (Milan, Rome, Florence, Venice) you can also tap a contactless card directly at the reader.

Useful Italian words at the station

  • Convalidare / obliterare — to validate
  • Binario — platform
  • Biglietto — ticket
  • Controllore — conductor / ticket inspector
  • Ritardo / Soppresso — delayed / cancelled

FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need to validate my train ticket in Italy?

Only paper regional tickets without a fixed time must be stamped in a machine before boarding. High-speed tickets (Frecciarossa, Italo) and Intercity tickets do not.

Do I need to validate a Frecciarossa or Italo ticket?

No. These tickets are for a specific train, date and seat, so no validation is needed.

What happens if I forget to validate my train ticket in Italy?

You can be fined on board. Find the conductor immediately and explain before they check you.

How do I validate a digital Trenitalia regional ticket?

Open the ticket in the Trenitalia app and tap Check-in before boarding, if the ticket is not already tied to a specific train.
