Colosseum and Vatican tickets: how to book

The two most booked sights in Rome, explained simply: which ticket to buy, where to buy it and how to skip most of the queue.

Updated 14 November 2026 · 6 min read · By the 687 team

The short answer

Book both online before you leave, on the official sites or through a reseller, and choose a morning slot. The standard Colosseum ticket costs €18 and also covers the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill; the Vatican Museums cost €25 online and include the Sistine Chapel. All tickets are in your name: bring the ID or passport you booked with.

  • Official tickets are sold on ticketing.colosseo.it and are released about 30 days ahead. The Full Experience sells out first.
  • Under 18s enter free, but must collect their ticket at the ticket office.
  • Sold out on the official site? Look for Colosseum tickets and tours on Tiqets: they cost more but often still have slots.

Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel

  • Price: €20 plus a €5 online booking fee, so €25. The Sistine Chapel is included.
  • Hours: Monday to Saturday 8:00–20:00, last entry 18:00. Closed on Sundays, except the last Sunday of the month.
  • Only official site: tickets.museivaticani.va. Tickets open about two months ahead and summer slots sell out weeks before.
  • Dress code: shoulders and knees covered, also for St Peter's Basilica.
  • No official slot left? Skip-the-line Vatican tickets on Tiqets are the usual plan B.
St Peter's Basilica is a separate visit and entry is free; you only pay to climb the dome. Expect a security queue, shorter early in the morning.

Free days

  • Vatican Museums: free on the last Sunday of the month, 9:00–14:00 (last entry 12:30), with very long queues.
  • Colosseum: free entry on some first Sundays of the month, with tickets to be collected on the day; check the official calendar.

Free days save money but cost hours in line: on a short trip, paying is usually worth it.

How to plan the day

Never put both on the same morning. A good plan: Colosseum and Forum on one morning, Vatican Museums and St Peter's on another, and an easy afternoon in between. To walk to both, sleep in Monti (Colosseum) or Prati (Vatican): compare hotels in Rome.

Tips

  • Beware of fake "official" sites that resell at higher prices: check the web address before paying.
  • Arriving from the airport? Read Fiumicino to Rome. Planning more cities? See Rome, Florence and Venice in 7 days.
  • In summer, bring water and visit the Forum early: there is little shade.

Prices and hours are indicative and can change — always check before you travel.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How much is the Colosseum ticket?

In 2026 the standard 24-hour ticket costs €18 and includes the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill. The Full Experience tickets with the underground or the upper levels cost €24.

How much are the Vatican Museums tickets?

€20 plus a €5 online booking fee, so €25. The Sistine Chapel is included.

Do I need ID for the Colosseum and the Vatican?

Yes. Tickets are in your name, so bring the passport or ID you used to book.

Can I visit the Colosseum and the Vatican on the same day?

It is possible but tiring. It is better to do them on two different mornings.
