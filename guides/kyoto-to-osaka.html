Kyoto to Osaka by train: which one to take

Four train lines link the two cities in 15 to 50 minutes. The right one depends less on speed than on where you sleep and where you are going.

By Gabriel P. · Updated 16 November 2026 · 6 min read

The short answer

Staying near Kyoto Station? Take the JR Special Rapid (shinkaisoku): about 29 minutes to Osaka Station for ¥580, every 15 minutes or so, covered by the JR Pass. Staying in Gion or Kawaramachi? Take the Hankyu from Kyoto-kawaramachi: about 43 minutes to Osaka-umeda for around ¥410. No reservation is needed on either: just tap in with your IC card.

Which train from where

  • Kyoto Station area → JR Special Rapid to Osaka Station (Umeda). For Universal Studios, change at Osaka for the JR Yumesaki Line.
  • Gion, Kawaramachi, Nishiki Market → Hankyu from Kyoto-kawaramachi or Karasuma to Osaka-umeda.
  • Fushimi Inari, east Kyoto → Keihan: Fushimi-Inari station is on the same line, so you can visit the shrine on the way.
  • Going to Namba / Dotonbori → arrive at Umeda (JR or Hankyu) and take the Midosuji subway line south, or take Keihan to Yodoyabashi, which is on the same subway line.
  • Kansai Airport → the JR Haruka limited express runs direct from Kyoto Station; no need to stop in Osaka.

JR Special Rapid: the default

  1. At Kyoto Station, follow the signs for the JR Kyoto Line towards Osaka. Check the board for Special Rapid (新快速): local and rapid trains cost the same but take longer.
  2. Tap in with ICOCA, Suica or PASMO, or buy a ¥580 ticket at the machine.
  3. It stops at Shin-Osaka (~23 min) and then Osaka Station (~29 min).
Avoid the Haruka for this hop: it is an airport limited express and needs a supplement. The Special Rapid is just as practical to Osaka.

Hankyu: the cheapest, from central Kyoto

Limited Express trains leave from Kyoto-kawaramachi (next to Gion and the Kamo river) and Karasuma, reaching Osaka-umeda in about 43 minutes. Hankyu Osaka-umeda is a separate building from JR Osaka Station, a few minutes' walk away. On weekends some departures are the Kyo-train Garaku, a sightseeing train with tatami-style decor at no extra fare.

Keihan: for Gion and Fushimi Inari

Keihan runs along the east side of Kyoto (Demachiyanagi, Sanjo, Gion-shijo, Fushimi-Inari) to Kyobashi and Yodoyabashi in Osaka, in about 50 minutes. It is the most scenic choice if you combine the trip with Fushimi Inari, and Yodoyabashi is handy for Kitahama and Nakanoshima.

Shinkansen: only if it fits

The bullet train takes only about 15 minutes, but it costs around ¥1,450 and arrives at Shin-Osaka, outside the centre. It makes sense if you have a JR Pass (Hikari and Kodama), if you continue to Hiroshima or Tokyo, or if you are already at the Shinkansen gates. For Osaka itself, the Special Rapid is usually just as fast door to door. More in our Shinkansen guide.

Tips

  • Avoid 7:30–9:30 on weekdays with big suitcases: all lines are commuter trains. Or send bags ahead with takkyubin from hotel to hotel.
  • One IC card works on all four lines, the Osaka subway and buses.
  • Kyoto and Osaka are close enough for a day trip either way. Plan the days with Kyoto in 2 days and Osaka in 2 days.
  • Want rooms near the station? Compare hotels in Umeda and Namba.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How long is the train from Kyoto to Osaka?

About 29 minutes on the JR Special Rapid from Kyoto Station to Osaka Station, about 43 minutes on the Hankyu from Kyoto-kawaramachi, about 50 on the Keihan, and about 15 minutes on the Shinkansen to Shin-Osaka.

How much is the train from Kyoto to Osaka?

¥580 on the JR Special Rapid, around ¥410 on the Hankyu and around ¥490 on the Keihan. The Shinkansen costs about ¥1,450 with a non-reserved seat.

Do I need to book the Kyoto to Osaka train?

No. JR Special Rapid, Hankyu and Keihan trains need no reservation: tap in with an IC card such as ICOCA, Suica or PASMO.

Is the Kyoto to Osaka train covered by the JR Pass?

The JR Special Rapid and the Hikari and Kodama Shinkansen are covered. Hankyu and Keihan are private railways and are not.
