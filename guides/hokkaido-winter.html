Hokkaido in winter: the complete guide

Powder snow, giant ice sculptures, a canal lit by candles, penguins walking in the snow and hot springs at −10°C: Japan's northern island is at its most magical between December and February. Here is how to plan it.

By Gabriel P. · Updated 18 November 2026 · 9 min read

The short answer

Go in early February if you want the festivals: the Sapporo Snow Festival runs 4–11 February 2027 and the Otaru Snow Light Path runs 6–13 February 2027, so 6–11 February gives you both. Base yourself in Sapporo, do Otaru as an evening trip, give one long day to Asahikawa's penguin walk and Biei, and add drift ice or an onsen if you have more time. Book hotels now: festival week sells out months ahead.

Sapporo: the winter capital

During the Snow Festival the long Odori Park fills with huge snow sculptures (some as big as buildings), projection-mapping shows at night and food stalls; Susukino has the ice sculptures and Tsudome is for families with snow slides. All three sites are free. Outside festival week Sapporo is still worth it: the Mt Moiwa ropeway for the night view, the old Sapporo Beer Museum and, above all, food — miso ramen, soup curry and jingisukan (grilled lamb). Susukino, in the photo above, is the place to eat after dark.

Festival tip: go to Odori after sunset on a weekday. Weekends and the last day (11 February, a national holiday) are extremely crowded.

Otaru: the canal by candlelight

A small port town about 35–45 minutes from Sapporo by JR. In the day: the canal, the old warehouses, glass workshops, the music box museum and the freshest sushi on the island. During the Snow Light Path the canal is lined with floating candles and snow lanterns, and the old Temiya railway line becomes a path of lights. Arrive at dusk, eat, walk, and take a late train back — or sleep in Otaru to see it calm in the morning.

Asahikawa, Biei and Furano

At Asahiyama Zoo in Asahikawa, king penguins go for a winter walk through the snow among the visitors, usually from mid-December to mid-March, around 11:00 and also 14:30 (from March often only in the morning). It depends on snow and the animals: the zoo posts the day's times at the entrance. Asahikawa is about 1h25 from Sapporo by limited express.

Nearby, Biei becomes a silent white landscape of lone trees and rolling hills; the Blue Pond and the Shirahige waterfall are illuminated on some winter evenings. Furano is a great ski town with fewer foreigners than Niseko. Roads are icy: unless you have real snow-driving experience, take a day tour from Sapporo instead of renting a car.

Drift ice, onsen and powder

  • Drift ice (ryūhyō): from late January to early March, sea ice from Siberia reaches the Okhotsk coast. Icebreaker cruises leave from Abashiri and Monbetsu; the Shiretoko peninsula is wilder still. It's far: plan at least two days or fly to Memanbetsu.
  • Onsen in the snow: Noboribetsu (Hell Valley, about 1h from New Chitose) and Jozankei (an hour from Sapporo) are the easiest. An outdoor bath while it snows is the Hokkaido experience.
  • Powder: Niseko is world-famous and international (and expensive); Rusutsu, Kiroro and Furano are quieter. Sapporo Teine is 40 minutes from the city.

A 6-day plan (early February)

  1. Day 1: land at New Chitose, train to Sapporo, ramen and Snow Festival by night.
  2. Day 2: Snow Festival by day (Odori, Susukino), Mt Moiwa at night.
  3. Day 3: Otaru: canal and sushi, Snow Light Path at dusk.
  4. Day 4: Asahikawa: penguin walk, then Biei (tour or overnight in Asahikawa).
  5. Day 5: Jozankei or Noboribetsu onsen, or a ski day at Teine.
  6. Day 6: Shopping at Sapporo station, train to the airport.

Clothes and tips

  • Shoes matter more than coats: waterproof boots with non-slip soles. Clip-on ice grips are sold in convenience stores and stations.
  • Layers: thermal underwear, fleece, a long down jacket, hat, gloves. Shops and trains are hot inside, so you'll take layers off a lot.
  • Snow can cancel trains and flights: keep a buffer day before your flight home.
  • Sapporo has a big underground walkway between the station and Susukino: use it when it's −10°C.
  • Book festival hotels early: compare Sapporo hotels.
  • Coming from Tokyo? Flying is much faster than the train: see our Tokyo in 3 days guide and add Hokkaido at the end.

FAQ

Quick answers.

When is the Sapporo Snow Festival 2027?

From 4 to 11 February 2027 at the Odori, Susukino and Tsudome sites. Entry is free.

Can I see the Sapporo Snow Festival and the Otaru Snow Light Path together?

Yes: the Otaru Snow Light Path runs 6–13 February 2027, so between 6 and 11 February both are on. Otaru is about 35–45 minutes from Sapporo by train.

When is the penguin walk at Asahiyama Zoo?

Usually every day from mid-December to mid-March, around 11:00 and 14:30, if there is enough snow. From March it is often only in the morning; check the board at the entrance.

How cold is Hokkaido in winter?

In Sapporo in January and February it is usually below zero all day, and colder inland (Asahikawa) and on the Okhotsk coast. Waterproof, non-slip boots are essential.
