Tokyo to Kyoto: Shinkansen, bus or flight?

Three ways to cover the most popular route in Japan, compared door to door — with the seat that gets you the Mount Fuji view.

Updated 12 November 2026 · 6 min read · By the 687 team
IN THIS GUIDEShort answerCompare optionsBy ShinkansenBy highway busBy planeBig luggageTips

The short answer

Take the Nozomi Shinkansen: about 2 hours 15 minutes from Tokyo Station to Kyoto Station, trains every few minutes, around ¥14,000 with a reserved seat. On a tight budget, a night bus saves a hotel night. Flying is almost never worth it: Kyoto has no airport.

By Shinkansen (Tokaido line)

  1. Board at Tokyo Station or Shinagawa (handy for southern Tokyo); Shin-Yokohama is a third option.
  2. The Nozomi is the fastest; the Hikari stops more often; avoid the Kodama, which stops everywhere (~3h40).
  3. Buy tickets at JR ticket machines, the ticket office or online with the SmartEX app (works with foreign cards).
  4. Reserved seats are worth it in peak periods (Golden Week, Obon, New Year, cherry blossom season).
Mount Fuji view: going from Tokyo to Kyoto, sit on the right side — seat E in ordinary cars (D in Green Car). Fuji appears around 40–45 minutes after Tokyo, near Shin-Fuji, on clear days.

Using a JR Pass? It covers Hikari and Kodama. For Nozomi you need an extra special ticket for the journey. See Is the JR Pass worth it? and the full Shinkansen guide.

By highway bus

  • Day buses and night buses (yako bus) leave from Busta Shinjuku and the Tokyo Station area, and arrive at Kyoto Station.
  • Seats range from basic 4-across to wider 3-across seats with curtains: worth the small extra on night buses.
  • Book a few days ahead; weekends and holidays sell out.

By plane

Kyoto has no airport. You would fly Haneda → Osaka Itami (about 1h10), then take a bus or train to Kyoto (about 1 hour). With airport check-in and transfers it is slower than the Shinkansen and rarely cheaper.

Big luggage on the Shinkansen

Suitcases with total dimensions (height + width + depth) over 160 cm and up to 250 cm need a seat with an oversized baggage area, booked in advance at no extra charge. Without the booking you pay a fee on board. Small suitcases go on the overhead rack.

Easier still: send big bags with takkyubin (luggage forwarding) from your Tokyo hotel to your Kyoto hotel, usually delivered the next day.

Tips

  • Pay for Kyoto city buses and subway with Suica or PASMO — the same card you used in Tokyo.
  • Planning Osaka too? Shinkansen Kyoto → Shin-Osaka is only ~15 minutes. See our Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka in 7 days.
  • Ekiben (station bento) from Tokyo Station make the trip part of the experience.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How long is the Shinkansen from Tokyo to Kyoto?

About 2 hours 15 minutes on the Nozomi, about 2 hours 40 on the Hikari and about 3 hours 40 on the Kodama.

How much is the Shinkansen from Tokyo to Kyoto?

Around ¥14,000 one way with a reserved seat on the Nozomi. Non-reserved seats cost a little less.

Which side to sit on for Mount Fuji from Tokyo to Kyoto?

The right side: seat E in ordinary cars, seat D in the Green Car.

What is the cheapest way from Tokyo to Kyoto?

Highway buses, from around ¥3,000 one way, but the trip takes 7–9 hours.
