Tokyo to Hakone: how to get there and the Freepass

Hakone is the closest onsen and Mount Fuji area to Tokyo: about 80 minutes by train. Here is which train to take, whether the Hakone Freepass pays off and how to do the classic loop.

By Gabriel P. · Updated 17 November 2026 · 7 min read

The short answer

From Shinjuku, take the Odakyu Romancecar limited express straight to Hakone-Yumoto: about 80 minutes, all seats reserved, no change. One way costs about ¥2,470 (¥1,270 fare + ¥1,200 seat ticket). If you stay near Tokyo Station or have a JR Pass, the Shinkansen to Odawara (about 35 minutes) plus the local Hakone Tozan train (about 15 minutes) is faster. If you will ride the ropeway, boat and buses, buy the Hakone Freepass.

Is the Hakone Freepass worth it?

The Hakone Freepass (Odakyu) covers the return Odakyu fare from Shinjuku and unlimited rides on the Hakone Tozan train, cable car, Hakone Ropeway, Lake Ashi sightseeing cruise and most local buses for 2 or 3 days. From Shinjuku it costs ¥7,100 (2 days) or ¥7,500 (3 days); from Odawara ¥6,000 / ¥6,400. The Romancecar seat ticket is not included: add ¥1,200 each way.

Rule of thumb: if you do the full loop (train, cable car, ropeway, boat, bus), the Freepass pays for itself, because the ropeway and cruise alone cost over ¥3,000 one way. If you only go to an onsen in Hakone-Yumoto and back, buy normal tickets. It is sold on the Odakyu/EMot website and app and at Odakyu stations; it cannot be loaded on a Suica or Pasmo. You can also book it online in advance.

The Hakone loop, step by step

  1. Hakone-Yumoto → Gora on the Hakone Tozan mountain train (switchbacks through the forest, about 40 minutes).
  2. Gora → Sounzan by cable car (about 10 minutes).
  3. Sounzan → Owakudani → Togendai on the Hakone Ropeway: volcanic vents, black eggs and, on a clear morning, Mount Fuji.
  4. Togendai → Moto-Hakone on the Lake Ashi cruise; walk to the Hakone Shrine torii in the water.
  5. Moto-Hakone → Hakone-Yumoto by bus (about 35–40 minutes), then the train back to Tokyo.
Check before you go: the ropeway stops in strong wind and during maintenance, and Owakudani can close when volcanic gas levels rise. Replacement buses usually run. Check the official Hakone Navi notices the day before. For alerts in Japan, see Japan safety alerts.

With a JR Pass

The JR Pass covers the Shinkansen Tokyo → Odawara (Kodama and Hikari, not Nozomi), but not the Odakyu Romancecar or the transport inside Hakone. With a pass, ride to Odawara and buy the Freepass from Odawara (¥6,000). More in Is the JR Pass worth it? and the Shinkansen guide.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Boarding the Romancecar with only a Freepass or IC card: you also need the seat ticket. Weekends and autumn leaves season sell out, so book it early.
  • Doing the loop backwards in the afternoon: Fuji is clearest in the morning, and queues for the ropeway and boat grow from 11:00.
  • Dragging suitcases around the loop: send them ahead with a luggage delivery service (takkyubin) or leave them in coin lockers at Hakone-Yumoto.
  • Trying it all as a rushed day trip: one night in a ryokan with dinner and onsen is the real Hakone.
  • Tattoos: many public onsen still refuse them. Look for tattoo-friendly baths or book a room with a private bath.

Tips

  • Sit on the right side of the Romancecar from Shinjuku for the best chance of seeing Fuji near Odawara; the front observation seats of the GSE train sell out first.
  • Hakone and the Fuji Five Lakes are different areas: for Kawaguchiko see Mount Fuji day trip.
  • Pay small fares with a Suica or Pasmo if you do not buy the Freepass.
  • Want an onsen in your room? Compare ryokan in Hakone.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How long does it take from Tokyo to Hakone?

About 80 minutes on the Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto, or about 35 minutes by Shinkansen from Tokyo to Odawara plus 15 minutes on the local train.

How much is the Romancecar to Hakone?

About ¥2,470 one way from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto: ¥1,270 fare plus a ¥1,200 reserved seat ticket. With a Hakone Freepass you only pay the ¥1,200 seat ticket.

Is the Hakone Freepass worth it?

Yes if you do the Hakone loop (mountain train, cable car, ropeway, Lake Ashi cruise, bus): it costs ¥7,100 for 2 days from Shinjuku and the ropeway and cruise alone are over ¥3,000. Not for a simple onsen trip to Hakone-Yumoto.

Does the JR Pass cover Hakone?

Only the Shinkansen from Tokyo to Odawara (Kodama or Hikari). The Romancecar and the transport inside Hakone are not covered: buy the Freepass from Odawara.
