Privacy Policy — 687 Italy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

687 Italy ("the App") is a travel companion app for tourists visiting Italy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and what choices you have. We built the App to work with as little personal data as possible.

1. Information We Access

Location

The App can request access to your device's location to show nearby restaurants, attractions, shops, and practical services (parking, fuel, ATMs, hospitals, stations) via Google Places, and to center maps on your position. Location is requested only when you use these features, is not requested automatically on app launch, and is not stored on our servers. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings; the App will continue to work without it, using manually selected cities instead.

Microphone

The App can access your device's microphone only if you actively tap the microphone icon in the translator, to convert your speech to text for translation via Google Translate. Audio is not recorded or stored by the App.

Trip and Wallet Data

Information you enter yourself — such as trip dates, saved documents in the Travel Wallet, and itinerary details — is stored locally on your device (using browser local storage and IndexedDB) so the App can work offline. This data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers, and is not shared with third parties.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide flight, accommodation, car rental, train, and attraction search and booking, the App links to and communicates with the following third-party services. When you use these features, standard technical requests (such as search parameters) are relayed through our own proxy server to protect API credentials; we do not add personal profiling on top of what these providers independently collect under their own privacy policies:

  • Aviasales / Travelpayouts — flight search and booking links
  • Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com (via Stay22) — accommodation search and booking links
  • Booking.com Cars — car rental booking links
  • Italo, Trenitalia — train booking links
  • Tiqets, Klook — attraction ticket booking links
  • Google Places, Google Maps, Google Translate — nearby places, maps, and translation
  • Open-Meteo — weather data (no personal data sent)
  • exchangerate-api — currency conversion data (no personal data sent)

These services may use affiliate tracking identifiers to attribute bookings made through the App; these identifiers are not linked to your personal identity by us. We encourage you to review each provider's own privacy policy for details on how they handle your data once you leave the App to complete a booking.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact details
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising profiling

4. Rate Limiting and Security

To protect the App's paid API resources from abuse, technical rate-limiting is applied both on your device and on our server infrastructure (Cloudflare). This process only evaluates request frequency and does not involve collecting personal or identifying information beyond what is technically necessary to prevent abuse.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Trip data you create is stored only on your own device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data/cache in your device settings, or by using the in-app options to remove a saved trip. Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
